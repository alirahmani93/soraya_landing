# Photography brief

The enhancement pipeline can fix lighting, backgrounds and framing. It cannot
invent thread detail that was never captured, and it cannot recover a blown-out
red. Get these right in camera.

## Setup

- **Backgrounds:** two only — warm paper (#F6EFE2) and deep ink (#241C17). Shoot
  every subject on both if time allows; the site needs to place products against
  light and dark sections.
- **Light:** one main source, warm, directional, roughly late-afternoon in
  quality. A soft fill to open the shadows. Flat even light destroys the surface
  texture that makes saffron look expensive.
- **Exposure:** protect the reds. Saffron red clips before the rest of the frame
  does; expose for the stigma and lift the shadows afterwards, never the reverse.
- **Tripod and a fixed white balance** across the whole session. Matching colour
  in post across a session that drifted is far more work than getting it right.
- **Shoot RAW.** The enhancement step wants headroom.
- **Include a scale reference** in at least one frame per subject.

## Practical

- Clean every surface between shots. Loose saffron dust on a dark background is
  visible at full resolution and tedious to retouch out of twenty frames.
- Handle saffron with tweezers. Fingers leave oil and it shows.
- Shoot the drawers before the components — the drawers must look untouched, and
  once product has been handled for macro work it no longer does.
- Keep a written log of which frame is which. Twenty near-identical drawer
  positions are impossible to sequence afterwards from memory.

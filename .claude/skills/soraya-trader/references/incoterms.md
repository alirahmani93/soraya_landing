# Incoterms for the Zanjan → Oman lane

Name the term on every quote. An unnamed term means the buyer read DDP and you
wrote EXW, and the difference is the entire export cost rung.

## The four that matter here

**EXW (Ex Works, Zanjan)** — buyer takes it from the farm gate. Lowest price on
paper, maximum friction for the buyer: they must arrange Iranian inland transport,
export clearance and the origin documents, which a Muscat trader will not want to
do. Use only for a buyer who already runs Iranian supply lines.

**FCA / FOB (named Iranian port or airport)** — you handle inland transport and
export clearance including the certificate of origin and phytosanitary
certificate; the buyer takes it from there. This is usually the right default:
the origin paperwork is yours to get anyway, and it is the part the buyer cannot
do.

**CIF / CIP (Sohar or Muscat)** — you also pay freight and insurance to the
Omani port. Simpler for the buyer, and it lets you control freight quality, which
matters for a product that degrades with heat and light. Duty and clearance remain
theirs.

**DDP (buyer's warehouse)** — you carry everything including Omani duty, VAT and
clearance. Only quote this if the Omani family company is the importer of record
and the duty rate is *confirmed*, not estimated. Quoting DDP against an unresolved
HS classification is quoting a number you do not know.

## Recommendation

Start at **CIP Muscat** with the Omani family entity as consignee, then sell
domestically within Oman. It puts the fragile part of the journey under your
control, keeps the customs exposure inside the family company where the import
licence sits, and lets you quote Omani buyers a clean domestic price with no
border in the middle of the conversation.

Quote **FCA** as the alternative for a buyer who insists on running their own
freight.

## Freight mode

Saffron is high value, low weight, and degrades with heat, light and humidity.
Air freight is the default; the freight cost per box is small against the goods
value, and the transit-time reduction protects the product. Sea only makes sense
at volumes this business will not see for some time, and then only with proper
temperature consideration.

Whatever the mode: airtight, opaque, and sealed at origin. Once opened for
inspection at a border, a saffron consignment does not go back to how it was.

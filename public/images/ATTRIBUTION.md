## What is deliberately missing

**There is no photograph of the box**, because the box does not exist yet. The
drawer cube on the landing page is drawn in CSS from the brand palette rather
than photographed or AI-generated. That is the honest option: a stock photo of
someone else's packaging would misrepresent the product, and a generated one
would advertise something that does not ship.

Replace it with the real thing once the box is manufactured — the imagery skill's
shot 4 (fixed-camera stop-motion of the drawers opening) is what the scroll
sequence wants.
